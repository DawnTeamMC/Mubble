---
title: Controls
---

[Platformer movement]($platformer_movement/overview) has its own **controls**, in the *Platformer Movement* category of the controls menu (Options → Controls → Key Binds). They can be rebound without changing the vanilla controls.

The basic platformer controls default to the same keys as their vanilla counterparts, and the controls menu does not show them as conflicting. Only one set works at a time: while platformer movement is on, the vanilla movement, *Jump*, *Sneak*, *Sprint*, *Attack* and *Use Item* controls do nothing and the platformer ones take over; the rest of the time, the platformer controls do nothing. All the other controls, such as the inventory, the hotbar or Mubble's *Trigger Power-Up*, work as usual.

## Keyboard and mouse

| Control | Default | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Move Forward, Move Backward, Move Left, Move Right | W, S, A, D | Move, relative to the camera |
| Jump | Space | Every [jump]($platformer_movement/moves#jumps) |
| Jump (second key) | Not bound | A second *Jump* control, for controllers |
| Crouch, Ground Pound | Left Shift | Crouch on the ground, [ground pound]($platformer_movement/moves#ground-pound) in the air |
| Sprint | Left Control | Reach the running speed right away, swim faster |
| Attack · Roll, Dive in a combo | Left Button | Attack, or [roll]($platformer_movement/moves#roll) with *Crouch* held and [dive]($platformer_movement/moves#dive) during a ground pound |
| Use Item · Roll, Dive in a combo | Right Button | Use the held item, or roll and dive as *Attack* does |
| Spin | B | [Spin]($platformer_movement/moves#spin) |
| Recenter Camera | Not bound | Swing the [camera]($platformer_movement/camera) behind the player, level with the horizon, with the player in the middle |
| Platformer Debug HUD | Not bound | Show technical details about the player's movement |

Quickly turning the movement keys all the way around, within half a second (for example W, D, S, A in a row), also spins.

## Controllers

With the [Controlify](https://modrinth.com/mod/controlify) mod installed, every platformer control also has a controller binding, in the *Platformer Movement* category of Controlify's controls. By default, they are laid out as in *Super Mario Odyssey*.

The platformer bindings only work while platformer movement is on and no menu is open. Meanwhile, Controlify's own bindings on the same buttons are paused; the rest of the time, they work as usual. Controlify's own defaults are left unchanged, so playing without platformer movement feels the same as before.

| Control | Xbox | PlayStation | Switch | Paused Controlify binding |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Move | Left stick | Left stick | Left stick | Movement |
| Jump | A | Cross | B | Jump |
| Jump (second key) | B | Circle | A | Mubble's *Trigger Power-Up* |
| Crouch, Ground Pound | RT | R2 | ZR | Attack |
| Attack · Roll, Dive in a combo | X | Square | Y | Swap Hands |
| Use Item · Roll, Dive in a combo | Y | Triangle | X | Inventory |
| Sprint | Left stick press | L3 | Left stick press | Sprint |
| Recenter Camera | Right stick press | R3 | Right stick press | Sneak |
| Trigger Power-Up (platformer layout) | LT | L2 | ZL | Use |
| Inventory (platformer layout) | D-pad up | D-pad up | D-pad up | Open Chat |
| Spin | Not bound | Not bound | Not bound | |
| Toggle Camera Follow (controller) | Not bound | Not bound | Not bound | |
| Platformer Debug HUD | Not bound | Not bound | Not bound | |

- The left stick is analog: tilting it partway walks, and tilting it all the way runs.
- As in *Super Mario Odyssey*, *Spin* has no button: quickly turning the left stick all the way around, within half a second, spins.
- *Trigger Power-Up (platformer layout)* and *Inventory (platformer layout)* bring back the two actions whose buttons the platformer layout takes.
- *Toggle Camera Follow (controller)* turns the [camera following the player]($platformer_movement/camera#following-the-player) on or off. It can also be added to Controlify's radial menu.
- Everything else, such as the hotbar on the bumpers, dropping items, the radial menu and pausing, stays where Controlify puts it.

### Button guide

Controlify's button guide shows what each button would do at that moment: *Jump*, then *Double Jump* right after landing, *Long Jump* or *Backflip* while crouching, *Ground Pound* in the air, *Dive* during a ground pound, *Roll* on *Attack* and *Use Item* while crouching, *Hold On* on *Crouch* while climbing, and so on.

### Rumble

Moves make the controller rumble lightly: a short tap for jumps, a thud for impacts. Only the player performing the move feels it, and only while playing on a controller. Controlify's own rumble settings can scale it down or turn it off.

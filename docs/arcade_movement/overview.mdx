---
title: Arcade movement
---

**Arcade movement** is a way of moving around inspired by 3D platformers such as *Super Mario Odyssey* and *Donkey Kong Bananza*. It gives the player a set of acrobatic [moves]($arcade_movement/moves) (chained jumps, backflips, long jumps, ground pounds, dives, rolls, wall jumps, ledge grabs and spins) and an [orbit camera]($arcade_movement/camera) that turns freely around them.

It stays Minecraft: the player keeps the same hitbox, collides with the same blocks and plays by the same rules as everyone else. A player who does not have arcade movement moves exactly as in vanilla.

## When it applies

Arcade movement drives the player as soon as something gives it to them. Which moves are available depends on what gave it; walking, falling and landing are always available.

Vanilla movement takes over for as long as the player:

- is dead or sleeping
- rides an entity, such as a boat, a horse or a minecart
- is in Spectator mode
- glides with an elytra
- flies in Creative mode
- is in lava
- changes dimension

Arcade movement picks up again on its own once none of these apply. Water and ladders are not on the list: arcade movement has its own [swimming and climbing]($arcade_movement/swimming_and_climbing).

## Profiles

How arcade movement plays depends on its **profile**, which comes with whatever gives arcade movement. Mubble comes with two:

| | Overworld | Trial |
|---|---|---|
| Made for | Survival | Minigames and parkour maps |
| Running speed | 6.8 blocks/s | 8 blocks/s |
| Jump heights | 90% | 100% |
| Food | Moves cost food | Free |
| Fall damage | Starts 3 blocks later than in vanilla | None |
| Interactions | Everything vanilla allows | Fighting and using items only: blocks and entities cannot be mined, placed or used |

For comparison, vanilla walking is 4.3 blocks/s and vanilla sprinting 5.6 blocks/s.

## Food

With the Overworld profile, moves cost food the way vanilla actions do. Each move adds some exhaustion when it starts, and running adds 0.08 exhaustion per block, a little less than vanilla sprinting.

The speed moves (running, rolls, roll jumps, long jumps, dives and slides) need more than 6 hunger points (3 drumsticks), as sprinting does. With 6 or less, the player only walks.

> [!NOTE]- Exhaustion of each move
> | Move | Exhaustion |
> |---|---|
> | Jump, ground pound, spin, vault | 0.05 |
> | Double jump | 0.07 |
> | Triple jump, backflip, side somersault, ground pound jump, wall jump, dive, roll, roll jump, swim dash | 0.1 |
> | Long jump | 0.2 |
> | Running | 0.08 per block |
>
> In vanilla, a jump costs 0.05, a sprinting jump 0.2 and sprinting 0.1 per block.

## Fall damage

With the Overworld profile, fall damage starts 3 blocks later than in vanilla: after a 6-block fall instead of 3. With the Trial profile, falls never hurt.

Some landings never hurt, whatever the profile:

- the end of a [ground pound]($arcade_movement/moves#ground-pound) or a [dive]($arcade_movement/moves#dive)
- a [roll]($arcade_movement/moves#roll) started right as the player lands
- a bounce on a [slime block]($arcade_movement/items_and_blocks#blocks)
- [swimming and climbing]($arcade_movement/swimming_and_climbing)

## Status effects

| Effect | With arcade movement |
|---|---|
| Jump Boost | Every jump is 35% higher per level |
| Slow Falling | The player falls no faster than 2 blocks/s |
| Levitation | The player rises as in vanilla |
| Speed, Slowness | The running speed changes as the vanilla walking speed does |

Depth Strider and Dolphin's Grace work as in vanilla, see [Swimming]($arcade_movement/swimming_and_climbing#swimming).

## Animations and effects

Moves animate the whole player, for everyone to see: flips and spins, the body stretching on take-offs and squashing on landings. The model eases from one animation into the next instead of snapping. Walking, running and crouching keep the vanilla animations.

Moves also play sounds and particles, which every player nearby sees and hears. On a controller, they make it [rumble]($arcade_movement/controls#rumble) lightly.

## Multiplayer

Arcade movement works the same in single player and on servers. The server checks every move against the player's input, and sends back a player whose movement does not match.

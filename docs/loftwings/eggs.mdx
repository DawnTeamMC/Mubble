## Mushroom spores

Hold a **glass bottle** to a mushroom or a nether fungus and it fills with **mushroom spores**. It takes the mushroom with it, so keep a farm if you plan to raise birds.

Spores are the only thing a [loftwing]($loftwings/overview) eats. Feed one to a hurt bird to heal it, and feed two healthy birds to breed them.

## Laying

Loftwings are birds: a pair does not produce a chick on the spot. One of them lays a **loftwing egg** where it stands, and the egg does the rest.

## Hatching

An egg only ever changes at **midday**, and then only one time in twenty. It goes through three stages, so on average a little over twenty days pass between the laying and the chick:

1. Loftwing egg
2. Cracked loftwing egg
3. Hatching loftwing egg

Once the third stage comes good the egg disappears and a chick is left in its place. Its breed is drawn like any other, so a nest can turn up a crimson loftwing.

## Moving an egg

Break an egg with anything ordinary and there is nothing left to pick up. **Silk Touch** returns the egg at the stage it had reached, so you can move a nest without losing the time already spent on it.

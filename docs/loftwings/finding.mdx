## Where they live

A [loftwing]($loftwings/overview) only ever appears on solid ground **above the cloud level**, in any biome of the Overworld. In practice that means mountain peaks, and whatever you have built up there yourself: at sea level you will never meet one.

Once it has hatched or spawned, a loftwing goes up. Left alone it glides in wide, slow circles above the clouds, and it only comes down for a reason.

## What it does all day

| | |
|---|---|
| **Soaring** | Its default, and nearly all of its time: lazy circles above the clouds. |
| **Roosting** | At nightfall it looks for the highest open perch nearby, lands and sleeps there until dawn. Hurt it, or crowd it, and it wakes up and leaves. |
| **Fishing** | By day it now and then drops onto open water and comes back up with a fish, which it swallows a few seconds later. A hurt loftwing fishes far more often: that is how it heals itself. |
| **Preening** | On the ground, especially straight out of the water, it stands still and runs its beak through its feathers. |
| **Curiosity** | It comes down to circle a stranger and look them over. This is how it meets people, and the only reason anyone ever gets [chosen]($loftwings/bond). |

Riding it, sitting it down or leashing it puts a stop to all of this.

## Breeds

Loftwings come in five breeds. They differ only in colour, and in how often they turn up.

| Breed | Rarity |
|---|---|
| Blue | Common |
| Green | Common |
| Yellow | Common |
| Violet | Common |
| **Crimson** | One in eighty-one |

The crimson loftwing is the breed the people of Skyloft believed extinct until Link's showed up, and it is as rare here.

A naturally spawned loftwing has a 5% chance of bringing a chick along.

title: The bond
---

A [loftwing]($loftwings/overview) is not tamed. There is no food you can hold out, no number of attempts that wears it down. **It decides.**

## Being chosen

Whenever a wild loftwing has somebody within eight blocks, it makes up its mind about them. One time in four it takes to them, and that person is its owner from then on. The other three times it turns them down — and a refusal is **final**. The bird remembers that face and will never consider them again, no matter how often they come back.

Walk up to a loftwing you have already been refused by and it simply ignores you. Find another one, and you get another roll of the dice.

When a loftwing chooses you, it teaches you its call: you are handed a [loftwing whistle]($loftwings/flying#calling-your-loftwing).

## Being refused

It is not all bad. A loftwing that turned you down and later sees you flying past on one of its own kind will, now and then, drop you a **chainmail loftwing helmet**. That is the only way to get one: it cannot be crafted.

## Your loftwing

Your own loftwing:

- can be sat down and stood up again by crouching and using it, like a dog or a cat
- **never teleports to you**. Lose it and it stays where it is, beating its wings and calling out until you come looking — or until you blow the [whistle]($loftwings/flying#calling-your-loftwing)
- fights back when something attacks it, with a beat of its wings that blows everything in front of it a couple of blocks backwards and barely scratches the attacker
- never joins a fight you picked

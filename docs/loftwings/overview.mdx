**Loftwings** are the giant birds of *The Legend of Zelda: Skyward Sword*, the mounts the people of Skyloft live with. They spend their lives above the clouds, they fly rather than walk, and they are the only animal in Mubble that cannot be tamed: a loftwing picks its own owner, and never changes its mind.
